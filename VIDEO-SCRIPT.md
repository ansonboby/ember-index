# Demo Video Script — 3:00 max (Space Apps requirement)

Record at 1080p, captions on, with a **pre-seeded app state** (Moon Base profile open,
simulator parked mid-run) so every take is clean. Do a full dry run twice before recording.
Upload public to YouTube or Vimeo; paste URL into `SUBMISSION.md` §6.

## 0:00–0:20 — Cold open
- **B-roll:** Saffire flame footage/stills from the challenge Resources tab; a candle
  burning normally vs. the microgravity blue sphere.
- **VO:** "In freefall, fire becomes a sphere — and NASA has spent two decades learning
  how it burns. An Artemis II commander put it bluntly: 'If you want to get somebody's
  attention really quick, make the fire alarm go off in your spacecraft when you're still
  about 80,000 miles from home.' Two decades of data, scattered across dozens of
  experiments. We turned it into decisions."

## 0:20–0:50 — Mission-profile mode (the hook)
- **Screen:** Rank tab, ISS profile selected. Click **Moon Base**. Ranking reorders live.
- **VO:** "This is Ember Index. Pick the environment crews will actually breathe — the
  Moon Base runs thirty percent oxygen at ten point two psi. That's forty percent more
  oxygen than Earth. Watch the evidence re-rank: which experiments matter for a Moon base,
  and why. Every score is a visible breakdown — weights are sliders. It's an auditable map
  of NASA's data, not a black box."

## 0:50–1:20 — Envelope chart (the money shot)
- **Screen:** Envelope tab. Dots = tested conditions; shaded bands = mission profiles.
- **VO:** "Every dot is a test NASA actually ran, in oxygen–pressure space. The shaded band
  is where crews will live. See the white gap? Nobody has burned anything at partial
  gravity. That gap is why LUCI and FM² must fly."

## 1:20–1:50 — Investigation card + sources
- **Screen:** Open the BASS (or Saffire) card; scroll the score breakdown; click the PSI link.
- **VO:** "Each card shows what the experiment tested — fuels, oxygen, flow, hardware — and
  every attribute links to its NASA PSI record. Validity you can audit."

## 1:50–2:30 — Simulator (the game)
- **Screen:** Simulator tab, pre-seeded rover run: set 30% O₂, budget spent on detection +
  suppression, event fires, SI explanation appears.
- **VO:** "And if numbers aren't your thing — play it. You're the Crew Safety Officer. Your
  rover cabin runs thirty percent oxygen, because that cuts pre-breathe time. But richer
  air ignites easier, and the data knows it. Choose your materials, detectors and
  suppressants; the engine rolls outcomes from NASA's flammability results — every rule
  cited, labeled illustrative. Here's the fire. Here's the experiment that predicted it."

## 2:30–2:50 — Ask the SI + Earth spin-offs
- **Screen:** Ask-the-SI tab, one typed question and its answer. Quick flash of the
  Earth-applications section (water-mist extinguishers, smoke detection, firefighter gear).
- **VO:** "Ask the SI anything about the dataset. And the science comes home — the same
  research built water-mist extinguishers and better smoke detectors here on Earth."

## 2:50–3:00 — Close
- **Screen:** Title card: **Ember Index** + repo/live URLs.
- **VO:** "Ember Index turns NASA's data into decisions for the next giant leap. Make it
  a safer one."
